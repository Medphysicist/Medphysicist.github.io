Saad Bin Saeed Ahmed

PhD Candidate, Medical Physics & Radiomics · Florida Atlantic University

Clinically trained medical physicist turned researcher. I build large-scale radiomics pipelines for feature extraction, validation and machine learning in radiation oncology, and I work on plastic scintillation dosimetry for fast commissioning and patient-specific QA in proton beam therapy. Ten years of hands-on clinical physics — treatment planning, linac and PET/CT commissioning, TG-51 / TG-142 / TRS-398 QA — across Pakistan and the United States.

// research software I build and maintain

Radiomics Workbench

open source · EUPL-1.2

An IBSI-conformant radiomics workbench for research imaging analysis, across modalities. Built against the Avanzo et al. (2026) robust-radiomics guideline, with a 106-item requirements register and automatic compliance scoring, so a study can show exactly which recommendations it meets.

  • IBSI-conformant feature extraction (MIRP), SUV and normalisation preprocessing
  • Segmentation review with ICC and kappa agreement statistics
  • Leakage firewall, volume-confounding screening, calibration and evaluation
  • Hash-chained audit logging for a complete, verifiable provenance trail
  • DICOM discovery, batch processing, and a Niivue-based viewer in the browser
  • Ships as a downloadable bundle with Linux, macOS and Windows launchers
